# 🎓 Study Mate | Your Ultimate Campus Companion

Welcome to **Study Mate**, a smart and simple platform built *for students, by students*. Whether you're cramming for exams, updating your resume, or looking for your college's latest resources — we've got you covered.

From **notes and past papers** to a slick **resume builder** and a robust **admin panel**, Study Mate is the one-stop solution every college student needs.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### 📂 For Students
- **📝 Download Notes & Papers**  
  Say goodbye to WhatsApp groups and endless scrolling. Find previous question papers and study notes organized by semester and subject.

- **📄 Build Your Resume**  
  Impress recruiters with a sleek, professional resume built right from your browser.

- **🏫 Explore College Info**  
  Get all the details about departments, events, faculty, and more in one place.

---

### 🛠️ For Admins
- **📥 Upload with Ease**  
  Manage papers, notes, and college updates with an intuitive admin panel.

- **🔄 Keep Content Fresh**  
  Update and maintain all academic content anytime, from anywhere.
